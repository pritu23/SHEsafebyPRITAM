Knowledge, Rights & Self-Defense

Vital knowledge every woman should have at her fingertips.

Tactic

Key Vulnerable Targets

  • Eyes & Nose: Palm heel strike upwards into the nose causes involuntary eye-watering and disorientation.
  • Throat / Windpipe: Direct strike to the base of the throat disables aggressive movement instantly.
  • Groin & Knees: Hard upward knee or heel stomp to the instep of the foot allows immediate escape.
Legal

Your Statutory Legal Rights

  • Zero FIR: A woman can file a police complaint (FIR) at ANY police station regardless of jurisdiction.
  • Arrest Protections: Women cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except under exceptional magistrate orders.
  • Identity Confidentiality: Victims of harassment/assault have statutory right to complete anonymity under law.
Protocol

Cab & Ride Safety Rules

  • Child Lock Check: Check the rear door child locks before closing the door to ensure you can open from inside.
  • Share Live Ride: Always share the in-app live tracking link with a trusted contact before starting the ride.
  • Phone Call Pose: Pretend or make a real call mentioning the cab number and ETA clearly within earshot of the driver.
